## Common questions

**The pick is not what I wanted.** Tap "Not quite, spin again" for the next best fit, or "Change my answers" to adjust the quiz.

**How many places are in the list?** 64 real Christchurch places and activities, and picks are filtered by the time of day you are asking.

**Are the hours always right?** Places change their hours and prices. Check before you head out.

**Does it need the internet?** No. The app works offline; only the maps link needs a connection.
