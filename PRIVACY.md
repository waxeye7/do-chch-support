# Privacy Policy for Do Chch

**Effective date: 31 October 2026**

Do Chch ("the app") suggests one thing to do in Christchurch. It is made by Merlin Robin Stemmer ("I", "me", the developer). This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Short version

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and I do not sell data.
- Your answers, picks and history stay on your phone.
- The app has no server of its own and sends nothing to me.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your quiz answers, the pick it showed you, and a short list of places it has already suggested, so it can avoid repeating itself. These are stored on your device and are never sent to me or to anyone else.

## What leaves your device

**Nothing goes to me.** Do Chch has no backend and makes no network requests of its own.

**Maps.** When you tap "Take me there", the app hands the place name to your maps app by opening a link. That app then handles the search under its own privacy policy. The app does not send your answers or your location to the maps app.

**App updates.** On iPhone, the standard app update check is handled by Apple through the App Store and by Expo's update service (expo.dev) when the app opens. Those requests carry the usual technical information needed to serve an update, such as the app version. They do not include your answers or picks.

## What the app does not do

The app:

- Has no account or sign-in
- Shows no ads and does not use advertising identifiers
- Does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data
- Does not include analytics or crash reporting
- Does not track you across other apps or websites
- Does not sell or share your data for advertising

Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may copy the app's saved data. That is handled by Apple or Google under their terms, not by me.

## Retention and deletion

Your answers, picks and history live only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing on my side to delete. If you have a question about your data, email the contact below.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Accuracy of suggestions

Places change their hours, prices and opening status. The app suggests, you check. Always confirm hours before you head out. Nothing in the app is a booking or a guarantee of entry.
